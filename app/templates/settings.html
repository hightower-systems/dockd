International shipping

When enabled, orders with a non-US destination route through the customs path: dockd adds a Commodities block + CustomsValue to the ShipRush XML. Per-item HS codes, country of origin, unit weight, and unit value must be populated on the Sentry item master for the labels to clear ShipRush submission.

Shipper tax IDs

Required by certain destination customs authorities. Leave blank if not registered. Admin-only; these are not exposed to non-admin sessions or the public settings endpoint.

Banned destinations

Hard-block list. A ship attempt to any country here is rejected by dockd before any ShipRush call. ISO 3166 alpha-2 codes, comma-separated. The OFAC comprehensive-sanctions defaults (CU, IR, KP, SY) are seeded on first boot; tune for your compliance posture. Operators cannot override this from the pack screen.

Secrets (.env)

Writes update .env and live os.environ; ShipRush/Sentry clients read the new values on the next request. Leave a field blank to keep its current value.
